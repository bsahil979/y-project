# Phase 8 frozen allocation replay protocol

Frozen before inspecting strategy outcomes on 16 November 2026. This is a retrospective chronological replay on an already inspected year, not a new untouched confirmatory test.

- Input: the exact five-stock adjusted-close snapshot and execution calendar from `regime_phase4_2026-11-15`. Holdout: 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31, 249 sessions. Rebalance every 21 sessions, 504-return estimation window, decision at close t, execution t+1, first return t+2. Simulate pre-holdout holdings to retain drift at the boundary.
- Costs: 10 basis points times total absolute traded weight, including initial purchase in the full replay; no final liquidation. Risk-free rate: 2% annually. Average turnover means mean absolute traded weight per scheduled rebalance in the holdout; also report daily average and annualized traded notional.
- Strategies: conservative (20% cap, volatility/beta objective), moderate (30%, maximum Sharpe), aggressive historical target-return control (40%, no AI), aggressive production-personalized AI Black-Litterman (40%, 10-year horizon), plus frozen equal-weight and 40%-capped minimum-variance baselines. The AI strategy is the aggressive pipeline path; target return is an explicit ablation, not production aggressive behavior.
- Fixed horizon 10 years isolates risk profiles. Short/medium horizons are not evaluated by this study. Conservative is forced to exactly equal weight in a five-stock universe; no claim of risk reduction against equal weight is possible here.
- MarketMind A architecture: 250-tree balanced Random Forest, seed 42, 14 existing features, no model selection on the holdout. Refit each rebalance on pooled five-stock observations in the prior 504-return window whose five-session labels have fully matured by the decision date. Labels use +/-1% five-session returns from the frozen price snapshot. No globally fitted or 2026 model is used.
- Features: close-derived technical indicators recomputed from the frozen snapshot with trailing calculations; causal shared HMM filtering. ADX is taken from the hashed historical technical-feature snapshot (OHLC not in the price snapshot). Its upstream implementation uses trailing OHLC indicators. No stored targets, future returns, regime codes or sentiments are consumed. This retained-feature dependency and retrospectively adjusted prices limit point-in-time data certification.
- Historical news is unavailable. Classifier sentiment feature is the existing neutral missing-value convention 0; BL FinBERT is None, never observed neutral. Record sentiment_coverage=unavailable_in_historical_replay. AI views use MarketMind probabilities only, with unchanged production signal/uncertainty rules.
- Failed constrained optimization stops the study, never substitutes an unconstrained allocation. Weight caps apply at rebalance; drift between trades may exceed them.
- Uncertainty: paired non-circular 21-session moving-block bootstrap, 10000 replicates, seed 20260927, 95% intervals for net Sharpe differences versus minimum variance and equal weight. Volatility and maximum-drawdown differences for conservative versus aggressive and equal weight use the same paired samples. No parameter tuning or outcome-based changes.
- Artifacts: input/source hashes, cached causal features, all per-rebalance probabilities and label maturity dates, allocation/view provenance, daily net returns, turnover, costs, metrics and bootstrap results. No trained production model files are overwritten.
